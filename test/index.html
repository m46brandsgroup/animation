SWITCHING FROM LUCID?

Questions traders ask before they move.

Do I have to give up my Lucid account to start?

No. Run a Tradeify Select account alongside your Lucid Flex account for as long as you want. Most switchers compare their first few payouts on each before deciding where to focus.

How are Select's Flex caps different from Lucid Flex caps?

Tradeify Select Flex caps are $2,500 on a 50K account and $4,500 on a 150K account, compared with Lucid Flex caps of $2,000 and $3,000 respectively. Both Flex plans offer payouts every 5 days. Tradeify's terms hold for the life of the account; Lucid Flex converts automatically to a different tier after 5 payouts.

How does the Daily payout plan handle drawdown?

End-of-day drawdown in both the evaluation and the funded account. Your intraday swings don't blow the account; only where you close the day matters. And once funded, the Daily path pays out every trading day.

What happens to my account after a few payouts?

Nothing changes. The account you buy is the account you keep, with the same terms, payout after payout. On top of that, consistency requirements on applicable plans relax after every payout you take.

How does the live program work?

Three approved payouts qualify you for Tradeify Elite. Your account moves to real, live capital, and consistent trading earns monthly rewards of up to $20,000 on a 50K account, on top of your 90% split. Lucid has no live program.

How fast are payouts, really?

Payouts process in hours, not cycles, and the denial rate is 0%. Every rule is published before you pay and nothing changes retroactively.
